## 2. Secondary / Conditioning Goals
- **Strength / Calisthenics:** <e.g., 5 strict unassisted dead-hang pull-ups, 20 clean push-ups>

---

## 3. Periodization Timeline
| Phase | Duration | Focus | Key Sessions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base Phase** | Weeks 1–6 | Aerobic capacity, tendon loading, volume ramp | Easy base miles, progressive long runs, strides |
| **Build Phase** | Weeks 7–12 | Lactate threshold, sustained tempo | 5x1km or 3x2km @ LT2, marathon pace blocks |
| **Peak Phase** | Weeks 13–16 | VO2 max, race-pace specificity | Race-pace intervals, short sharpeners |
| **Taper & Race** | Weeks 17–18 | Fatigue dissipation, carbohydrate loading | Sharp low-volume strides, tune-up test |
