# Biomechanical Safety & Tendinopathy Protocols

## 1. Tendon Loading Principles (Cook & Purdam Continuum)
- Tendons adapt slower than skeletal muscle due to lower vascularity and slower collagen turnover.
- **Compressive vs. Tensile Load Differentiation:**
  - **Insertional Achilles Tendinopathy:** Aggravated by **compressive load** (deep ankle dorsiflexion wrapping the anterior tendon fibers against the calcaneus). Perform all calf loading on a **flat floor (neutral ankle)**—never drop the heel below parallel off a step, and avoid deep knee-over-toe squats/crouching.
  - **Mid-Portion Achilles Tendinopathy:** Aggravated primarily by excessive tensile stretch-shortening cycle loads.
- **The 24-Hour Latency Rule:** Tendon stiffness and tenocyte fluid uptake typically peak 12 to 24 hours **after** mechanical stress, not during the run itself (due to the warming "analgesic effect" of exercise).

## 2. 3-Tier Readiness & Morning Stiffness Matrix (Green / Amber / Red)
Evaluate readiness by combining **7-day rolling HRV trend**, **Resting HR delta**, **ACWR (0.8–1.25)**, and **Morning Stiffness (0–10 scale)**:

* **🟢 GREEN LIGHT (Score 0–2 | 7-Day HRV Balanced | Normal RHR):**
  - Negligible morning stiffness lasting $< 5$ minutes. Proceed with full scheduled quality workout (`CV`, `LT2`, or Long Run).
* **🟡 AMBER LIGHT (Score 3 | Single-Night Sleep <75 | Mild Life Stress):**
  - Mild stiffness dissipating within 10–15 minutes, or isolated single-night sleep disruption.
  - **Apply Minimum Effective Dose (MED) Scaling Levers:** Do *not* reflexively cancel the session over single-night noise. Instead:
    1. Shift `10K / CV` pace down to controlled `Sub-Threshold (LT2)` with 60s walking rests, **OR**
    2. Trim total interval volume by ~20% (e.g., 4 reps instead of 5), **AND**
    3. Mandate high-drop ($\ge 8.5\text{ mm}$) trainers on flat tarmac.
* **🔴 RED LIGHT (Score $\ge 4$ | Multi-Day HRV Suppression | RHR $\ge +4$ bpm | ACWR $> 1.30$):**
  - Noticeable localized tenderness, crepitus, or stiffness lasting $> 20$ minutes, or systemic autonomic suppression.
  - **Mandatory Intervention:** Cancel quality intervals immediately. Convert to a flat Zone 1/2 recovery flush ($< 125$ bpm) in 10mm drop trainers or non-impact cross-training (cycling/swimming).

## 3. Heavy Slow Resistance (HSR), Analgesic Isometrics & Kinetic Chain
- **Daily Analgesic Isometrics (Skip Routine Icing):**
  - Perform **3–5 × 45-second single-leg neutral isometric calf holds** on flat ground for cortical motor-unit recruitment and tendon analgesia.
  - **Avoid Routine Post-Run Icing:** Unless acute retrocalcaneal bursitis swelling is present, do not routinely ice tendons post-run, as hypothermia blunts capillary perfusion and prostaglandin-mediated collagen synthesis.
- **Heavy Slow Resistance (HSR — 3x/week):**
  - **Exercises:** Weighted standing calf raises (gastrocnemius focus) and bent-knee calf raises (soleus focus) strictly on a flat floor.
  - **Tempo:** 3 seconds concentric up, 2 seconds isometric hold, 3 seconds eccentric lowering (3 sets × 12 reps).
- **Upstream Kinetic Chain (Hip & Glute Medius):**
  - Pair calf HSR with lateral hip/glute medius strengthening (clamshells, lateral band walks, single-leg RDL/balance) to prevent late-race pelvic drop and medial ankle valgus torque.

## 4. Velocity-Normalized Biomechanics (Cadence, GCT & Stride Length)
- Because $\text{Velocity} = \text{Cadence} \times \text{Stride Length}$, stride length naturally and safely expands with running speed via **posterior hip extension and elastic recoil** during flight phase (e.g., running `3:59 /km` at `180 spm` mathematically requires a **`1.40 m` stride length**).
- **Do NOT enforce arbitrary static stride-length caps** (such as `1.38 m`) at threshold or race paces.
- **True Overstriding Diagnostic:** Overstriding is landing anteriorly ahead of the center of mass (COM) with an extended knee. Flag overstriding/braking hazards **only** when stride extension is paired with:
  1. **Sluggish Velocity-Scaled Cadence:** `< 165 spm` on easy base runs or `< 175 spm` on threshold/interval runs.
  2. **Prolonged Ground Contact Time (`GCT`):** `> 275 ms` on easy runs or `> 245 ms` on speed/threshold runs.
  3. **Excessive Vertical Bounding (`Vertical Ratio`):** `VR > 9.2%` on easy runs or `> 8.5%` at threshold.
